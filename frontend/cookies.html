Cookies Policy

Last updated: April 1, 2026  ·  Effective: April 1, 2026

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how BrokerAI uses them, and the choices you have. By continuing to use our platform, you consent to our use of cookies as described here.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or phone) when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to re-enter settings every time you return.

Similar technologies include localStorage and sessionStorage, which store data within your browser. We use these to persist your authentication token and app preferences without requiring a server round-trip.

2. Cookies We Use

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the platform to function. Without them, features like logging in, staying authenticated, and scheduling posts in your local timezone would not work. You cannot opt out of these cookies while using the Service.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how users interact with BrokerAI — which features are popular, where users drop off, and what could be improved. The data is aggregated and anonymised. You can opt out by adjusting your browser settings.

We Do Not Use

BrokerAI does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels for ad targeting, or third-party marketing cookies. We do not share cookie data with advertisers.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some of our third-party integrations may set their own cookies:

  • Ayrshare: When you authenticate social accounts via Ayrshare's OAuth flow, Ayrshare may set session cookies in that window. These are governed by Ayrshare's Privacy Policy.
  • Tailwind CSS CDN: Our frontend loads Tailwind CSS from a public CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com). CDN requests may be logged by the CDN provider but do not set persistent cookies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Here's how for common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data
Note: Blocking strictly necessary cookies (like your authentication token) will prevent BrokerAI from functioning correctly and you will not be able to stay logged in.

To clear BrokerAI's localStorage data specifically, open your browser's developer tools (F12), go to Application → Local Storage → http://127.0.0.1:8000, and clear the entries.
