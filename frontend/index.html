Everything you need to know

You've got questions.
We've got answers.

Can't find what you're looking for? Talk to us — we respond fast.

What is BrokerAI and who is it for?
BrokerAI is an AI-powered social media campaign platform that works for any business — from solo content creators and small businesses to marketing agencies and enterprise teams. If you post on social media and want to spend less time on it, BrokerAI is for you.
How does AI generate my content?
BrokerAI uses a multi-step AI agent workflow powered by GPT-4 class models. When you start a campaign, agents work in sequence: a Strategy Agent builds your content plan, a Content Agent writes platform-specific captions and hashtags, a Media Agent generates image prompts, a Compliance Agent reviews everything, and a Scheduling Agent picks the best posting times.
Which social platforms are supported?
BrokerAI supports Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and TikTok. Publishing is handled via the Ayrshare API. Growth and higher-tier plans unlock additional platforms (up to 15 on Scale). Just connect your accounts once and all approved posts publish automatically.
Can I edit the AI-generated content before it goes live?
Absolutely. The Review page lets you read every post, edit any caption inline, check compliance flags, and approve or reject individual posts before anything is published. Nothing goes live without your approval.
What plans are available? Can I cancel anytime?
BrokerAI offers four plans — Starter ($29), Growth ($79), Pro ($259), and Scale ($399) — billed monthly with no long-term commitment. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings.
Is my data and content kept private?
Your content and account data are private to your account. We never share or sell your data. Campaign content is processed via OpenAI's API under their data usage policies. Social credentials are stored securely via Ayrshare and never exposed to our systems in plain text.
