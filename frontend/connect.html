Connect Social Media Accounts

Manage your Ayrshare connection so BrokerAI can publish approved posts to your linked social accounts.

Team workspace

Your team uses the owner’s connected social accounts for publishing. You do not need to connect your own profiles. If posts cannot publish, ask the team owner to connect (or reconnect) accounts on this page while signed in as them.

How to connect (2 steps)

  1. Tap Connect via Ayrshare below. You'll be redirected to Ayrshare to authorize your accounts (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and more).
  2. Once you've finished at Ayrshare, come back and tap Verify connection to confirm everything is linked.

What happens after connecting?

  • Approved campaign posts will automatically publish to your connected accounts on schedule.
